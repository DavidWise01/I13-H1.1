LANGUAGE LAYERS

Atoms → Syntax → Semantics.

The three layers answer different questions over the same program stream.

00 · ATOMSWhat pieces exist?
Names, constants, assignment, calls, arguments, comparisons, control and osmotic binding in minimal executable form.
01 · SYNTAXHow may the pieces be arranged?
Whitespace, grouping, multiline forms, nesting, desugared spellings and identifier/literal shapes accepted by lexer/parser.
02 · SEMANTICSWhat does an accepted arrangement mean?
Precedence, scope, binding resolution, equivalence, return/control meaning, kind boundaries and semantic rejection.
