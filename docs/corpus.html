GOLDEN CORPUS LAW

Flow, don't pile.

The corpus grows downstream. A later layer should consume the shape established upstream instead of becoming another disconnected bucket.

1 · CONTINUITYROCK[n].RIVER_IN == ROCK[n-1].RIVER_OUT. A broken handoff is a corpus failure.
2 · ONE DELTAEach rock introduces the smallest useful new executable behavior. Compound passages are allowed only when the behaviors cannot execute independently.
3 · TWO PROOFSEvery rock must agree on the reference VM and generated Wasm, then the composed river.i13 must traverse the current inside I13 itself.
