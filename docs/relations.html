I13 GOLDEN CORPUS · 03 RELATIONS · FLAY v0.1

Relations become method.

FLAY is a deterministic five-point fitting discipline: local origin, four cardinal relation probes, then closure at origin. New chunks can recurse into newly exposed space; old chunks can be re-used where a new relation makes them fit. Downstream remains blocked until the pass is satisfied.
