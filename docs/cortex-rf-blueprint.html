CONNECTIVITY CONTRACT

Netlist rule

Address nodes as L[x,y,z] and R[x,y,z], coordinates 0–2. Connect only Manhattan-distance-one neighbours. Center ports are L[1,1,1] and R[1,1,1].

Aᵢⱼ = 1 iff |xᵢ−xⱼ| + |yᵢ−yⱼ| + |zᵢ−zⱼ| = 1

Diamond ports: D.IN, D.OUT, D.PLUS, D.MINUS. Every passive connection must appear reciprocally in the extracted matrix.

ASSEMBLY ORDER

Build gates

  1. Simulate one LC tank; verify frequency and unloaded Q.
  2. Build a two-tank coupler; extract mode splitting 2J.
  3. Validate the four-port bridge independently for balance and quadrature.
  4. Populate one 3×3 layer, then the complete left 3³ lattice.
  5. Mirror the validated lattice for the right side.
  6. Attach both center ports and sweep all accessible ports.
  7. Freeze after measured S(ω) fits the extracted model.
