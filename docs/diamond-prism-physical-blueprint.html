PHYSICAL PHASE 01 · SIX-PORT DFT₃ NETWORK

BUILD THE
PRISM

The faithful electrical transpose uses three inputs and three outputs. One six-port network performs (P_3); a second conjugate six-port performs (P_3^dagger). Three phase lanes circulate between them through the diamond control boundary.

!

A passive, reciprocal, lossless network cannot also be matched at all three ports. The physical prism is therefore not one ordinary three-port splitter. It is a six-port (3\times3) beamforming network. This preserves the unitary target without hiding reflection or dissipation.

BUILD ORDER
  1. Synthesize the ideal six-port S matrix.
  2. Decompose it into couplers and phase shifters.
  3. Simulate schematic S parameters.
  4. Move to full-wave PCB simulation.
  5. Fabricate (P_3) only and measure all 36 S terms.
  6. Fabricate (P_3^dagger) after the first matrix passes.
  7. Connect three equal-delay torus lanes.
  8. Add the diamond routing plane and witness taps last.
