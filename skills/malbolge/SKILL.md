## What it is
**Malbolge** — an esoteric language by **Ben Olmstead (1998)**, named after
*Malebolge*, the 8th circle of Hell in Dante's *Inferno*. Deliberately
near-unprogrammable: a ternary VM (59,049 × 10-trit cells), 3 registers
(a, c, d), a "crazy operation", and **self-modifying code** (every instruction
is enciphered after execution). Epistemic status: VERIFIED (canonical spec).

## DISTILL — real spec (from fetched article)
- **8 instructions**, dispatched by `(([c] + c) mod 94)`:
  `4`=jmp [d], `5`=out a, `23`=in a, `39`=rotr [d], `40`=mov d,[d],
  `62`=crz [d],a, `68`=nop, `81`=end.
- **Crazy operation** — ternary 3×3 lookup table (real, in the tool).
- **Encipherment** — the 94-char permutation
  `9m<.TVac\`uY*MK'X~xDl}REokN:#?G"i@5z]&gqtyfr$(we4{WP)H-Zn,[%\\3dL+Q;>U!pJS72FhOA1CB6v^=I_0/8|jsb`
  (used to self-modify [c] after each step).
- **History:** first program was NOT human-written — generated by Andrew Cooke's
  Lisp beam-search. Lou Scheffer proved Turing-completeness + wrote a cat program.
  Malbolge proper is memory-limited (NOT Turing-complete); *Malbolge Unshackled*
  is the Turing-complete variant.
